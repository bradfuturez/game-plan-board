How to use

Add a card: press Add card, or double-tap an empty spot.

Edit a card: tap it. Move it: press and drag.

Tie red string: tap a card's gold pin, then tap another card. Cut a string: tap it (you can Undo).

Move around: drag the empty board. Zoom: pinch with two fingers, or use the + and − buttons.

Your work saves itself on this phone as you go. Press SAVE to also put a copy file in your phone's Downloads folder. Use Open to load one of those files back.

Install: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
