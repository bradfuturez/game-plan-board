How to use

Menu: hold your finger on an empty spot for half a second. Choose New window to put a window there, or Import photos, videos, files to bring things in from your gallery.

Type: tap a window and start typing. Tap the empty board when you're done. Move a window: press and drag it.

Paragraphs: leave an empty line between paragraphs. Windows widen by themselves as you write more, so everything stays visible. To keep every line break exactly as typed, turn on More → Keep my line breaks.

Put a photo in a window: drag it onto the window (the window lights up) and let go. The window grows to fit. Drag it out again to take it back out. Tap a photo or video to see it big or play it.

Orange rope: double-tap a window (it glows orange), then double-tap another window. Tap the empty board to cancel.

Red string: tap a window's gold pin, then tap another window. Cut a string or rope: tap it and choose Cut (you can Undo).

Remove things: hold a window, photo or file for a menu.

Main Window: hold your finger on a window and answer Yes to Make Main Window?. It glows purple and gets a purple tab on top that says Main window of the web, so you can see the hub of a group of windows tied together. You can have more than one. Hold it again and choose Remove Main Window? to undo it. With a mouse, right-click a window.

Ropes under windows: where a rope or string passes under a window, a faint glowing line in the same colour shows through on top of that window, so you can follow it across a crowded board. Taps still go to the window.

Move around: drag the empty board. Zoom: pinch with two fingers, or use the + and − buttons.

Your work saves itself on this phone, photos and videos too. Press SAVE to also put a copy file in your phone's Downloads folder (it includes your photos and videos). Use Open to load one back.

Update to GitHub (in More) sends your whole board, photos and videos included, to your private GitHub so your assistant can read it. The first time, it asks for your passcode.

DirectShare (in More) is your mailbox from your bots: when they send you a note, photo, video or file, the DirectShare box glows red and More gets a red dot. Tap DirectShare and each item lands in its own window in the middle of the screen. Move them like any other window.

BotResponse (the tab at the bottom) glows yellow when a bot has changed its copy of your board. Tap it to see what each bot added, changed or removed, then answer Yes, it matches me or No. Tap a photo to see it full size; videos play right there; tap a file to open it. Your answer goes back to the bot.

Bot tabs (top of the screen, under the title): when a bot has changes waiting, it gets its own tab with a red number. Tap the tab to see that bot's new windows and changes, then tap Yes to put each one on your board or No to throw it away. The number goes down as you answer; the tab goes away when that bot has nothing left. Nothing pops up on the board by itself.

Which window does the bot mean? In a bot's list (a bot tab or BotResponse), tap the white part of a card (not the Yes / No buttons). The list slides away, the board moves to that window and it glows orange for a few seconds. For a Tie you see both windows (and the rope, if it's already tied). Tap Back to Bot Responses (or your phone's back button) to return to the same card and answer Yes or No.

Game time line (the tab at the top, next to the window count) opens a separate view of your story: every window as a small card in era lanes, from earliest on the left to latest on the right. Windows with no era yet are in Unplaced. Hold a card for a moment, then drag it to another era. Swipe to move around, pinch to zoom. Tap a card to jump back to the board with that window glowing orange. Moving cards never moves or changes your windows on the board; the time line is saved to your private GitHub on its own. Tap Board (or your phone's back button) to go back.

BotBoardShare (hold an empty spot, right under New window; also in More) shows the boards your bots share with you. They are the bots' own boards, separate from yours. Pick one and the whole screen switches to it: move around, zoom, tap a window to read it, tap a photo to see it. A banner at the top says whose board it is; tap Back to my board to come home. Bot boards are view only, and looking at one never changes your board. If a bot's board has eras, Lanes shows it as a time line. A red dot means a bot shared or updated a board you haven't opened yet.

Install: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
