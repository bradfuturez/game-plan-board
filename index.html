How to use

Menu: hold your finger on an empty spot for half a second. Choose New window to put a window there, or Import photos, videos, files to bring things in from your gallery.

Type: tap a window and start typing. Tap the empty board when you're done. Move a window: press and drag it.

Paragraphs: leave an empty line between paragraphs. Windows widen by themselves as you write more, so everything stays visible. To keep every line break exactly as typed, turn on More → Keep my line breaks.

Put a photo in a window: drag it onto the window (the window lights up) and let go. The window grows to fit. Drag it out again to take it back out. Tap a photo or video to see it big or play it.

Orange rope: double-tap a window (it glows orange), then double-tap another window. Tap the empty board to cancel.

Red string: tap a window's gold pin, then tap another window. Cut a string or rope: tap it and choose Cut (you can Undo).

Remove things: hold a window, photo or file for a menu.

Move around: drag the empty board. Zoom: pinch with two fingers, or use the + and − buttons.

Your work saves itself on this phone, photos and videos too. Press SAVE to also put a copy file in your phone's Downloads folder (it includes your photos and videos). Use Open to load one back.

Update to GitHub (in More) sends your whole board, photos and videos included, to your private GitHub so your assistant can read it. The first time, it asks for your passcode.

Install: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
